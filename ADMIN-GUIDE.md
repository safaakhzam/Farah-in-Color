# Farah's guide to the dashboard

Everything here works from your phone. No coding needed.

## Logging in

1. Go to **yourwebsite.com/admin** (there is also an "Artist login" link at the very bottom of every page).
2. Enter your email and password, and tap **Log in**.
3. Forgot your password? Tap **Forgot your password?**, enter your email, and tap the link we email you. Then set a new password in **Settings → Change password**.

Tip: add the dashboard to your phone's home screen (Safari: Share → *Add to Home Screen*; Chrome: ⋮ → *Add to Home screen*).

---

## Adding a new painting

1. Tap **Paintings → Add a painting**.
2. **Photos:** tap **Add photos** and pick from your gallery. You can choose several at once.
   - The **first photo is the main one** shown in the shop. Use the arrows to reorder.
   - Under each photo, write a short description (e.g. *"Saffron Tide, a golden seascape in acrylic"*). It helps blind visitors and Google. If you leave it empty, one is created from the title.
   - Photos are resized automatically, so big phone photos are fine.
3. **Details:** title, price in dollars, width and height in cm (leave empty if you don't know yet), year, medium and a description.
4. **Filters on the shop page:** pick a collection and style (start typing to reuse existing ones), tick the main colors.
5. Tick **Feature on the home page** for your favourite pieces. The first available featured painting becomes the big picture at the top of the home page.
6. Tap **Save painting**. It's on the website immediately.

**Photo tips:** shoot straight on, in daylight near a window, without flash. Crop to the edges of the canvas. Add a second close-up photo of the texture.

## Editing or deleting a painting

**Paintings** → tap **Edit** next to a painting → change anything → **Save painting**.

To delete: open it, tap **Delete**, then tap again to confirm. This cannot be undone. If a painting was sold, it's usually nicer to keep it with the status *Sold*, so visitors see your past work.

## Painting status

| Status | What visitors see |
|---|---|
| **Available** | Price and the **Order** button |
| **Reserved** | "Reserved" badge, no Order button. Set automatically when someone orders. |
| **Sold** | "Sold" badge, no price, no Order button |

You can change the status right from the **Paintings** list with the dropdown. It saves instantly.

---

## When an order comes in

1. You receive an **email** with the customer's details and a green **"Message the customer on WhatsApp"** button. The painting is automatically **Reserved**, so nobody else can order it.
2. Open **Orders** in the dashboard (new orders show a red number in the menu).
3. Contact the customer with the **WhatsApp** or **Call** button to agree on a delivery day.
4. Update the status with the dropdown:

| Set the order to… | When | What happens to the painting |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed** | You agreed on delivery with the customer | Stays *Reserved* |
| **Delivered (paid)** | You delivered it and collected the cash | Becomes **Sold** |
| **Cancelled** | The customer changed their mind / couldn't be reached | Goes back to **Available** and can be ordered again |

Each order shows the **amount to collect in cash** (painting + delivery).

## Commission requests

**Commissions** lists custom painting requests with size, colors, budget, the idea and any reference photos (tap a photo to see it full size). Reply by email or WhatsApp, then set the status: *In discussion*, *Accepted*, *Declined* or *Completed*.

## Messages

**Messages** has everything sent from the Contact page and from "Inquire about this piece". Tap **Reply** to answer by email, then **Mark as replied** to keep things tidy.

---

## Settings

- **Delivery fee:** the flat fee across Lebanon (set to 0 for always free).
- **Free delivery from:** e.g. 500 means orders of $500 or more ship free. Leave empty to never offer free delivery.
- **WhatsApp number:** with 961, e.g. `961 3 123 456`. All WhatsApp buttons on the site use it.
- **Email for new orders & messages.**
- **Change password.**

Changes are live as soon as you tap **Save**.
